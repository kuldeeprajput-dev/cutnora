# Cutnora Code of Conduct

Cutnora welcomes people who want to improve the project, learn, and help others.
Everyone participating in its community is expected to treat others with
respect, regardless of background, identity, disability, experience, or skill.

## Where this applies

This policy applies to the Cutnora repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, code reviews, and other project-managed community spaces. It also
applies when someone is officially representing Cutnora elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work respectfully, including when you disagree.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and allow others time to respond.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different experience.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and cooperate in resolving conflicts.
- Respect people's privacy and their right to stop a conversation.
- Share only media, code, and other material you have permission to publish.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, discrimination, or targeted insults.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual comments and content.
- Publishing private information or recordings without permission.
- Repeatedly contacting someone after they have asked you to stop.
- Spam, impersonation, deliberate disruption, or abusive review comments.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or helps investigate one.

Good-faith disagreement, bug reports, and constructive criticism are welcome.
Participation does not require agreement with a maintainer's technical choices.

## Reporting a concern

Send conduct reports privately to the project maintainer, Kuldeep Rajput, at
[contact.kuldeeprajput@gmail.com](mailto:contact.kuldeeprajput@gmail.com).
Do not open a public issue containing an incident report,
private information, or allegations about another participant.

Include links or dates, a brief description of what happened, and any evidence
you are comfortable sharing. You may explain how you would prefer to be
contacted. Reports will be handled discreetly; information will be shared only
when necessary to investigate, respond, or meet applicable obligations.

You can also use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse occurring on its platform.
These reports are handled by GitHub, separately from project moderation.

## How concerns are handled

The maintainer is responsible for applying this policy fairly. Before taking
action, they will consider the available evidence, the effect on participants,
and whether the behavior is repeated. Someone with a conflict of interest
should not be the sole decision-maker for that report.

Depending on severity, actions may include requesting a correction, issuing a
warning, removing content, restricting participation temporarily, or banning
a participant. Serious threats or repeated harassment may result in an
immediate restriction without an earlier warning.

Where appropriate, the person affected by a moderation decision will receive
an explanation and may request a private review through the same reporting
contact. Reports about maintainers are covered by this policy too; GitHub's
reporting tools remain available for platform abuse.
